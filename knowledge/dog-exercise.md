Most adult dogs do best with at least two walks a day plus some play. Exact needs vary a lot with breed, age and health, so treat these as a starting point and ask your vet about any limits. Mental games such as sniffing walks, fetch and puzzle toys tire a dog out as well as physical exercise does.
