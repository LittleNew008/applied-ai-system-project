Most pets should see a vet for a check-up at least once a year, and more often when young, old or unwell. Keep vaccinations and parasite prevention (fleas, ticks, worms) up to date as your vet advises. Contact a vet promptly for repeated vomiting or diarrhoea, difficulty breathing, not eating or drinking, a swollen belly, or sudden changes in behaviour. A quick weekly look over your pet makes changes easier to spot. This is general information, not a diagnosis; your vet's advice comes first.
